Applications Engineering

The team between Innoflight hardware and the customer's system. A ticket comes in, the issue gets reproduced on a bench here, and then the real question: has this shown up in our own test history before?

HEARD ON A CALL
A recent case involved a unit reading about 7% off consistently across all of its data. It passed everything. Nobody saw it until someone graphed it out.
The workflow today

What we heard

Described by an Innoflight applications engineer, August 2026.

  • Customer tickets are divided across the applications engineers, four to eight in a week, some resolved in a day, some requiring testing that runs a month or two.
  • Much of the work is reproducing the customer's issue on Innoflight hardware to see whether it's real.
  • When it reproduces, the next step is going back through past test data and graphing it manually to check whether the same trend appears in history.
  • Big failures get caught. Small consistent deviations don't. They surface only when someone happens to plot them.
  • Findings get relayed to the integration and test team, who report that combing their test history takes significant time.
